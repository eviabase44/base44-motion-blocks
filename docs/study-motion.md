# Base44 study motion restored

The user rejected the smooth refinement because it no longer resembled the Base44 motion study. The earlier source-based Figma adaptation is restored as the default. Finesse must preserve the reference's timing language; it does not authorize replacing it with generic easing.

The camera stays frontal and deviceless, moving from the whole app into its features and back out to capabilities. The bundle is the main motion device; hard cuts are the default join grammar. The user's 19-second score, app palette/font, Figma compositions, soundtrack and final credit take precedence over historical defaults for other campaigns.

## Evidence and limits

The review used the recovered Base44 Motion v1 skill, its typography/motion overview and decision record, plus the later audit's scoped corrections. Relevant principles are held type (`05-R11`, `03-R7`), entry from speed (`03-R1–R4`), and hard layout changes (`04-R1`). The audit cautions against treating an overall easing summary or passing tests as fidelity proof.

Local source evidence was revisited: B44-06's opening word states, B44-07's native-frame feature window/type states, and all 26 delivery poses of the existing native SH22 render. The collage data in `src/data/collage.js` comes from pixel measurements of that render; the capability track in `src/data/reference-tracks.js` comes from B44-05. No source footage or private kit assets are redistributed by this change.

This is the previous **adaptation to the user's Figma layouts**, not a claim of a newly frame-exact recreation of an entire Base44 film. It retains its documented retargeting, slot-duration mapping, source fitting and predicted hover behavior. Changing playback to 12 fps alone would not restore those paths or event types; the renderer and its default parameters are restored together.

## Controls and persistence

**Base44 study · 12 fps** is the fresh-session default. **Restore Base44 study motion** applies its timing and hard-cut defaults to all eight shots, preserving every layer's position, size and visibility adjustment. Save, Load, Download, Import, per-shot timing, cut controls and direct canvas editing remain available. User-selected alternative cuts remain permitted by the earlier explicit request.

The smooth experiment remains accessible for old preset compatibility, clearly labeled **archived**. Loading an explicit or saved preset does not silently rewrite it. Use the restore button, then **Save tweaks**, to replace that app's saved motion.

Verification covers default/preset persistence, geometry, cut choices, editable layers, existing motion landmarks and the unchanged 19-second duration. Technical checks do not award a creative score or imply user approval.
